Motormounts for 6mm bearings. Both types of Bearings can be used, Regular and Flanged

MF63 bearing 3x6x2.5mm - Flanged Bearing
MR63 bearing 3x6x2.5mm - Regular Bearing

If using non flanged bearings, car will be around 0.6mm narrower per side, adjust that with shims

40/8 for 40t spur / 8t pinion - 1 dot
40/9 for 40t spur / 9t pinion - 2 dots
40/10 for 40t spur / 10t pinion - 3 dots
